# Lab 09: Triage an Outlook Inbox and Draft a Controlled Reply

**Topic 03** · **Intermediate** · **Outlook + Excel + Copilot** · **K2 · A3**

## Goal

Use Copilot to summarise, prioritise and draft without bypassing recipient and fact checks.

## Expected output

Inbox triage register, verified thread summary and one approval-ready draft reply.

## Files

- `Lumina-Living-Lab-09.xlsx` — learner starter and control workbook
- `completed/Lumina-Living-Lab-09-completed.xlsx` — completed comparison copy
- `LAB-09-Instructions.pdf` — printable detailed guide
- Other Office files in this folder are specific to this lab

## Primary prompt

> Using the Inbox_Register and the selected email thread, produce: (1) a five-line summary, (2) decisions and unanswered questions, (3) priority with rationale, and (4) a draft reply that acknowledges the issue, answers only supported points, assigns next actions and requests missing information. Do not send. Flag any recipient, date or attachment that requires verification.

## Procedure

1. **Open the Inbox_Register and review the priority rules.**

   Confirm VIP, deadline, risk and dependency fields.

2. **Select one supplied thread in Outlook or the lab message pack.**

   Do not use a live personal mailbox for this exercise.

3. **Generate a thread summary.**

   > Using the Inbox_Register and the selected email thread, produce: (1) a five-line summary, (2) decisions and unanswered questions, (3) priority with rationale, and (4) a draft reply that acknowledges the issue, answers only supported points, assigns next actions and requests missing information. Do not send. Flag any recipient, date or attachment that requires verification.

4. **Verify people, dates, commitments and attachment names.**

   Record each check in Draft_QA.

5. **Refine tone for the recipient.**

   > Make the reply concise, constructive and accountable. Preserve all verified facts and open questions.

6. **Run the human-send gate.**

   Check To, Cc, subject, commitments, confidentiality and attachments.

7. **Save the draft without sending.**

   Record Draft ready and the approver in the workbook.


## Acceptance test

The draft remains unsent, all facts and recipients are checked, and the triage register shows priority, owner and next action.

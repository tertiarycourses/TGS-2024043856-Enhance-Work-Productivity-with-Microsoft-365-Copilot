# Lab 03: Refine a Responsible Copilot Use Policy in Word

**Topic 01** · **Intermediate** · **Word + Excel + Copilot** · **K1 · A1**

## Goal

Use Word Copilot to consolidate review feedback while retaining governance controls and an audit trail.

## Expected output

Approved policy draft, issue log and staff quick-reference checklist.

## Files

- `Lumina-Living-Lab-03.xlsx` — learner starter and control workbook
- `completed/Lumina-Living-Lab-03-completed.xlsx` — completed comparison copy
- `LAB-03-Instructions.pdf` — printable detailed guide
- Other Office files in this folder are specific to this lab

## Primary prompt

> Revise the attached Responsible Copilot Use Policy using the Review_Log workbook. Resolve only items marked Approved. Preserve the sections on data classification, human review, prohibited inputs, attribution, incident escalation and record retention. Add a one-page staff checklist and a change summary table.

## Procedure

1. **Open the policy draft and Review_Log workbook side by side.**

   Sort the log by Decision and confirm the Approved count.

2. **Ask Copilot to list proposed changes before editing.**

   > Compare the policy with the Review_Log. List the exact edits required for rows marked Approved. Do not apply Pending or Rejected suggestions.

3. **Validate the proposed edits against the log.**

   Mark each approved item Ready or Needs clarification in the workbook.

4. **Request the controlled revision.**

   > Revise the attached Responsible Copilot Use Policy using the Review_Log workbook. Resolve only items marked Approved. Preserve the sections on data classification, human review, prohibited inputs, attribution, incident escalation and record retention. Add a one-page staff checklist and a change summary table.

5. **Review policy language for enforceability and plain language.**

   Replace vague words such as appropriate with a named owner, trigger or action where the source supports it.

6. **Record each accepted change and its source row.**

   Complete the Change_Evidence sheet.

7. **Save the revised policy as a new version.**

   Keep the original draft unchanged for comparison.


## Acceptance test

Every change traces to an Approved review row, all mandatory controls remain, and the policy includes a usable staff checklist.

## Evidence

- Completed workbook with Prompt_Log and Evidence_Register
- App-specific artifact and a credential-safe screenshot
- Verification, exception and human-approval note

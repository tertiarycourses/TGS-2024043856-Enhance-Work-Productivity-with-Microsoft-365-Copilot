# Lab 16: Operate a Governed End-to-End Copilot Pipeline

**Topic 04** · **Capstone Pipeline** · **Microsoft 365 Copilot agents + Outlook + Teams + Excel** · **K1-K4 · A1-A4**

## Goal

Operate the complete intake-to-delivery pipeline with approvals, exception handling, monitoring and staff handover.

## Expected output

End-to-end pilot run, approved Outlook draft, Teams handover, monitoring dashboard and staff training plan.

- `Lumina-Living-Lab-16.xlsx` — learner starter and control workbook
- `completed/Lumina-Living-Lab-16-completed.xlsx` — completed comparison copy

- Other Office files in this folder are specific to this lab

## Primary prompt

> Operate the Lumina Living campaign pipeline on the attached pilot cases. Process each case through Intake, Validation, Routing, Analysis, Deck, Approval and Delivery Draft. At every stage record status, owner, timestamp, evidence and exception. Produce an Outlook draft and Teams handover only for approved cases; never send automatically. Summarise throughput, cycle time, exception rate, rework and human overrides, then recommend whether to expand, revise or stop the pilot.

## Procedure

1. **Review the Pilot_Cases and Pipeline_Control sheets.**

   Confirm stage owners and approval rules.

2. **Process all pilot cases through intake and validation.**

   Incomplete or restricted cases must divert to exception handling.

3. **Run routing and analysis only for eligible cases.**

   > Operate the Lumina Living campaign pipeline on the attached pilot cases. Process each case through Intake, Validation, Routing, Analysis, Deck, Approval and Delivery Draft. At every stage record status, owner, timestamp, evidence and exception. Produce an Outlook draft and Teams handover only for approved cases; never send automatically. Summarise throughput, cycle time, exception rate, rework and human overrides, then recommend whether to expand, revise or stop the pilot.

4. **Create or validate the decision deck for one approved case.**

   Check charts, sources and limitations.

5. **Complete the human approval checkpoint.**

   Approver signs the Approval_Log before any delivery draft.

6. **Create an Outlook draft and Teams handover.**

   Verify recipients and keep both unsent/unpublished until trainer review.

7. **Calculate pilot KPIs in Excel.**

   Use formulas for throughput, median cycle time, exception rate, rework and override rate.

8. **Create the staff training and support plan.**

   Map role, task, risk, learning activity, evidence and support owner.

9. **Write the scale decision.**

   Choose Expand, Revise or Stop with evidence, guardrails and next review date.


## Acceptance test

All pilot cases have a complete audit trail, delivery remains human-controlled and the scale decision is supported by measured operational evidence.

## Evidence

- Completed workbook with Prompt_Log and Evidence_Register
- App-specific artifact and a credential-safe screenshot
- Verification, exception and human-approval note

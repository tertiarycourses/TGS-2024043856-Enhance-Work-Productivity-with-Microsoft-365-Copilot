## About the course

You follow the HR Shared Services team of **Lumina Living Pte Ltd**, a fictional Singapore home-living retailer. The same 120 synthetic HR cases (leave, training, onboarding, payroll and benefits requests across five departments, reporting date 30 September 2026) travel through every lab: Excel cleaning and checks, a Word HR service brief, PowerPoint and Outlook handoffs, Personalisation, workbook .Rules and reusable Skills, an interactive HR service dashboard and a professional management presentation.

Topic 4 builds the **Lumina HR Policy Agent** in Microsoft 365 Agent Builder: HR Policy Handbook knowledge plus the `hr-policy-qa` Skill, tested with eight known-answer and guardrail cases (cited answers, UNKNOWN, privacy, approval refusal, live data and conflicting sources) before named-user sharing and a tracked staff enablement plan.

The course closes with the **5 Principles of AI Security**: data control, permission control, human in the loop, traceability, and don't use the most powerful model.

## Learning outcomes

- LO1: Predict and mitigate challenges in integrating Microsoft Copilot to workflow.
- LO2: Develop a plan for implementing Microsoft Copilot to ensure seamless transition from legacy workflow.
- LO3: Evaluate opportunities in using Microsoft Copilot for Teams and Outlook.
- LO4: Design a training plan for staff to use Microsoft Copilot Studio.

## Topics covered

1. **Microsoft 365 Copilot Fundamentals** — Grounding, HR-case grain, prompts and verified handoffs (Labs 1–2)
2. **Skills, Rules and Personalisation** — Account preferences, workbook conventions and reusable methods (Labs 3–6)
3. **Create Interactive Dashboard and Professional Presentation** — Shared-scope measures, refresh tests, evidence story and reviewed handoff (Labs 7–10)
4. **Create Microsoft 365 Agents for Businesses** — Build a grounded HR agent: scope, knowledge, Skill, tests, sharing and staff enablement (Labs 11–14)

## Labs

Each lab folder holds an instruction PDF, a printable README, copy-ready prompts and a `data/` folder with synthetic inputs, templates and labelled rejoin checkpoints. Labs 1, 2, 3, 5, 6 and 11 also include a **Screen Guide**: real Microsoft 365 Copilot screenshots (chat pane, + menu, model and mode pickers, Outlook Work/Web, Personalization, the OneDrive skills folder, Custom skills, the @ Skill picker and All Agents) with numbered explanations. Topics 2 and 3 also show a worked Lumina HR Pareto chart and an interactive HR service dashboard, unfiltered and filtered to one department. Topic 4 (Labs 11–13) adds live Agent Builder screens: New agent, Configure, Knowledge sources, suggested prompts and a cited UNKNOWN test answer. The same screens are in the slides and Learner Guide.

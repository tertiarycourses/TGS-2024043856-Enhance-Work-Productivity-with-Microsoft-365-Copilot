Lumina Living Pte Ltd is a fictional Singapore home-living retailer whose HR Shared Services team handles employee HR cases. Request types are Leave Request, Training Request, Onboarding, Payroll Query and Benefits Claim, raised by employees in five departments (Retail Sales, Operations, Finance, Marketing, Technology) and handled by eight HR officers. Reporting date: 2026-09-30. HR cases have statuses New, Assigned, In Progress, On Hold, Closed, Cancelled. Backlog excludes Closed and Cancelled. Overdue means backlog due before the reporting date. Unassigned cases require a proposal, never an invented confirmed owner. Proposed actions need a manager decision. All sample data is synthetic.

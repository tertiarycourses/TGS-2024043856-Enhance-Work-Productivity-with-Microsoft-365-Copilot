# Lumina HR Policy Agent Staff Enablement Plan

## Roles
- Employee user asks HR questions, checks the cited section and reports wrong answers.
- HR reviewer verifies answers against the handbook and snapshot.
- Agent owner maintains instructions, knowledge, the hr-policy-qa Skill and tests.
- Knowledge owner (HR Director) approves handbook content and permissions.

## Learning route
1. Explain what the agent answers, its sources and its privacy and approval boundaries.
2. Demonstrate a cited leave answer and an UNKNOWN response.
3. Practise the eight test cases.
4. Use Microsoft Loop to record user, test, evidence, defect, owner and retest.
5. Share only with named users who already have access to the knowledge files.

## Transition and support
Pilot privately, test with named users, correct failures, then request controlled sharing. Keep the previous passing configuration and test log for rollback. Review monthly and whenever the handbook, knowledge, permissions or tenant policy changes.
